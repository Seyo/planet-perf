# Planet Districts — Design Reference

Districts form contiguous arcs around the planet. Each district has a **center** (peak density/height) and **edges** that blend into adjacent districts. Taller, denser buildings appear near the center; shorter, sparser structures appear near the edges.

## Transition Zones

When adjacent districts border each other, a transition slice range blends the two vocabularies:

| Transition | What to mix |
|---|---|
| Metropolis → Slums | Buildings shrink and decay; glass gives way to corrugated iron |
| Slums → Dirty Industry | Shacks replaced by small workshops, then full factory buildings |
| Clean Industry → Research Campus | Factories become labs; smokestacks become antenna towers |
| Toxic Wasteland → Ruins | Radiation signs mix with crumbled stone; dead trees throughout |
| Park → Cemetery | Trees thin out; gravestones appear; grass stays but darkens |
| Entertainment → Metropolis | Neon density drops; towers get taller and less decorated |
| Harbor → Logistics Hub | Water and cranes transition to dry inland warehouses and trucks |

### Density & Height Curve

Each district should apply a **bell curve** of height/density centred on the district midpoint:

- **Edge slices (outermost ±20%)** — sparse, short buildings, lots of gaps
- **Middle slices (±20–40%)** — medium density, moderate heights
- **Centre slices (innermost ±20%)** — maximum density, tallest buildings, most foreground detail layers
